# "They deleted it, we changed it" is silent

Carried from story 6b. When the other device deleted a note this one has since
changed, gitoxide calls it a resolution failure and we force it to ours. There
is no version of theirs to leave beside it, so nothing asks. Keeping someone's
writing is the safe direction; saying nothing about it is the gap.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A note they deleted and we changed is visible as a decision, not only
      kept quietly

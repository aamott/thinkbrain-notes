05

Alternatives considered

Three directions explored before landing on the mock above
Option A — Modal wizard
Pro: forces focus, one decision at a time.
Con: blocks the whole app and feels alarming — like an error dialog.
Option B — Side-panel only
Pro: lightweight, stays out of the way.
Con: cramped for long notes — hard to read a full mini-diff in a narrow rail.
Option C — Cards + full merge tab RECOMMENDED
Pro: plenty of room to read, non-blocking, matches editor mental model.
Con: more screens to build — a card list and a full tab view.

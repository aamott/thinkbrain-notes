- description: `DesktopPanelId = BuiltInDesktopPanelId | (string & {})` and `LeftPanel = RightPanel = DesktopPanelId`. `shellTypes.ts` re-exports these, so `DesktopShell`'s `useState<LeftPanel | null>` and `setLeftPanel`/`setRightPanel` now accept ANY string. Previously `LeftPanel` was the narrow union `"explorer" | "search" | "source-control" | "tags" | "extensions"` and `RightPanel` was `"outline" | "backlinks" | "properties" | "assistant"`, so a typo like `setLeftPanel("exlorer")` was a compile error. Now it compiles silently. The `(string & {})` trick is a common TS autocomplete pattern, but applying it to the shell's *selection state* (not just the registry's lookup key) discards the compile-time guarantee that the shell only ever selects registered built-ins. Combined with `getDesktopPanel` throwing in render (see related test-gap finding), a stray unregistered id persisted to `desktopState` would crash the render path. Recommendation: keep the registry's `id` field wide for future extensions, but retain a narrow `BuiltInLeftPanel`/`BuiltInRightPanel` union for the shell's `useState` and `selectLeftPanel`/`onToggleRightPanel` callbacks, widening only when extension panel selection is actually supported.

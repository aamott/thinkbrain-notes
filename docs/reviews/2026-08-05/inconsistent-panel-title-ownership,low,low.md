- description: The registry concentrates panel metadata (label, icon, factory) but the responsibility for rendering the panel's title header is inconsistent. `RightPopout` renders a single shared `<PanelTitle title={contribution.label} />` (line 51) above all keep-mounted contributions, and the right-side factories (`outline`, `properties`, `assistant`, `backlinks`) do NOT render their own `PanelTitle`. `LeftPopout` renders NO shared title, and each left-side factory renders its own `<PanelTitle>` (search lines 85, source-control line 99, tags line 112, extensions line 125) — except `explorer`, which renders none. A third-party extension author targeting the panel contribution point cannot tell from the contract whether to render a title. This is a maintainability/API-design smell that the formalization story should resolve: either the popout always renders the title from `contribution.label` (and factories must not), or each factory always renders its own (and the popout must not). Given the registry now owns `label`, the cleaner model is for the popout to render the title uniformly and remove the per-factory `PanelTitle` calls.
